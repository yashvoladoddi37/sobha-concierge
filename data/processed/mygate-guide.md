MYGATE GUIDE FOR SOBHA INDRAPRASTHA RESIDENTS

This guide covers common MyGate operations for residents of Sobha Indraprastha Apartments, Rajajinagar, Bangalore. MyGate is the official visitor and access management platform used by SIAOA.

1) PRE-APPROVE A VISITOR (GUEST, FAMILY, FRIEND)

To pre-approve a visitor so they can enter without calling you at the gate:

Step 1: Open the MyGate app on your phone.
Step 2: Tap "Pre-Approve" or the "+" button on the home screen.
Step 3: Select "Guest" as the visitor type.
Step 4: Enter the visitor's name and phone number.
Step 5: Set the expected date and time of arrival.
Step 6: Tap "Pre-Approve" to confirm.

The visitor will receive an OTP or a passcode via SMS. They share this code with the security guard at the Sobha Indraprastha gate for entry. You will also get a notification when the visitor arrives.

Note: If you do not pre-approve, the security guard will call you via MyGate for real-time approval. You can approve or deny from the app notification.

2) PRE-APPROVE A DELIVERY (SWIGGY, ZOMATO, BLINKIT, AMAZON, FLIPKART)

For food delivery (Swiggy, Zomato) and quick commerce (Blinkit, Zepto):
- Swiggy, Zomato, and Blinkit have direct integration with MyGate via Express Entry.
- When your order is picked up, the delivery platform automatically sends an entry request to MyGate.
- You will receive a MyGate notification asking you to approve the delivery executive's entry.
- Tap "Allow" on the notification to generate a gate passcode for the delivery person.
- The passcode is automatically shared with the delivery executive via the delivery app.

For e-commerce deliveries (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho):
- These platforms do NOT have automatic MyGate integration.
- You need to manually pre-approve the delivery person.
- Go to MyGate > Pre-Approve > Select "Delivery" > Enter the expected delivery date.
- Alternatively, when the delivery person arrives at the gate, the guard will call you via MyGate for approval.

Tip: If you are not home, you can still approve deliveries remotely from the MyGate app. The guard will hold the package or the delivery person can leave it at the gate as per your instructions.

3) PRE-APPROVE A CAB OR RIDE (UBER, OLA, RAPIDO)

Cab services like Uber, Ola, and Rapido do NOT have direct MyGate integration. You need to manually allow entry:

Option A — Pre-approve before the cab arrives:
Step 1: Open MyGate > Pre-Approve > Select "Cab".
Step 2: Enter the cab driver's name or vehicle number (available in your Uber/Ola app once a ride is booked).
Step 3: Set the expected pickup time.
Step 4: The driver will receive a passcode or the guard will let them through.

Option B — Approve when the cab arrives:
Step 1: When the cab reaches the Sobha Indraprastha gate, the security guard will create an entry request on MyGate.
Step 2: You will get a notification on your phone.
Step 3: Tap "Allow" to let the cab in.
Step 4: The guard raises the boom barrier.

Tip: If your cab is waiting outside and you are running late, you can approve entry from the app so the driver can wait inside the complex near your block.

4) APPROVE OR DENY ENTRY IN REAL-TIME

When any visitor, delivery person, or service provider arrives at the gate without pre-approval:

Step 1: The security guard scans or logs the visitor on the MyGate tablet at the gate.
Step 2: You receive a push notification on your MyGate app with the visitor's details (name, photo if available, purpose of visit).
Step 3: You can:
  - Tap "Allow" to approve entry.
  - Tap "Deny" to refuse entry. The guard will turn the visitor away.
  - Tap "Allow Once" for one-time entry or "Allow Always" to whitelist the person for future visits.

Important: If you do not respond within a few minutes, the guard may call you directly or deny entry by default. Keep MyGate notifications enabled on your phone.

5) MANAGE DOMESTIC HELP AND REGULAR VISITORS

For maids, cooks, drivers, nannies, and other daily staff:

Step 1: Open MyGate > "My Daily Help" or "Staff" section.
Step 2: Tap "Add Staff" and enter the person's name, phone number, and role (maid, cook, driver, etc.).
Step 3: Set the expected schedule (e.g., Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM).
Step 4: The staff member will receive a unique QR code or passcode for daily entry.
Step 5: They scan the code or share the passcode at the gate each day.

You will get an entry/exit notification each time your staff arrives or leaves. If your staff comes outside the scheduled hours, the guard will call you for approval.

To remove or change staff access:
- Go to MyGate > My Daily Help > Select the staff member > Tap "Remove" or "Edit Schedule".

6) RAISE A COMPLAINT ON MYGATE HELPDESK

For maintenance issues, common area problems, or facility complaints:

Step 1: Open MyGate > Tap "Helpdesk" or "Community" section.
Step 2: Tap "Raise a Complaint" or "New Ticket".
Step 3: Select the complaint category:
  - Plumbing
  - Electrical
  - Civil / Structural
  - Housekeeping / Common Area Cleaning
  - Security
  - Lift / Elevator
  - Parking
  - Noise Complaint
  - Garden / Landscaping
  - Other
Step 4: Describe the issue clearly. Include:
  - Your flat number and block (e.g., A-302)
  - Exact location of the issue (e.g., "3rd floor corridor near lift lobby, Block A")
  - How long the issue has been present
  - Any photos (tap the camera icon to attach)
Step 5: Tap "Submit".

The SIAOA facility management team will receive the ticket and assign it. You can track the status in MyGate under Helpdesk > My Complaints. You will get notifications when the status changes (Assigned, In Progress, Resolved).

For urgent issues (water leak, electrical hazard, lift stuck): Call the SIAOA management office directly at +91-77957 00320. Do not rely solely on the MyGate helpdesk for emergencies.

7) BOOK AMENITIES (CLUBHOUSE, BADMINTON COURT, PARTY HALL)

Sobha Indraprastha uses MyGate for amenity slot booking:

Step 1: Open MyGate > Tap "Amenities" or "Facilities" section.
Step 2: Browse available amenities:
  - Clubhouse
  - Badminton Court
  - Table Tennis
  - Party Hall / Community Hall
  - Swimming Pool (seasonal)
  - Gym
Step 3: Select the amenity you want to book.
Step 4: Choose an available date and time slot.
Step 5: Confirm the booking. Some amenities may require a refundable deposit or usage fee as per SIAOA rules.

Cancellation: You can cancel a booking from MyGate > Amenities > My Bookings > Cancel. Check SIAOA bylaws for cancellation policies and any applicable charges.

Note: Peak hours for badminton court and gym may have limited availability. Book in advance, especially on weekends.

8) PAY MAINTENANCE AND OTHER CHARGES

Monthly maintenance payments can be made through MyGate:

Step 1: Open MyGate > Tap "Payments" or "Dues" section.
Step 2: You will see your pending dues — monthly maintenance, sinking fund, or any special charges levied by SIAOA.
Step 3: Select the payment you want to make.
Step 4: Choose a payment method:
  - UPI (Google Pay, PhonePay, Paytm)
  - Net Banking
  - Debit/Credit Card
  - NEFT/RTGS (bank transfer details will be shown)
Step 5: Complete the payment. You will receive a receipt via MyGate and email.

For payment disputes or discrepancies, contact the SIAOA accounts team at accounts@siaoa.co.in.

Note: Late payment of maintenance charges may attract penalties as per SIAOA bylaws. Refer to the penalty schedule or ask about specific penalty amounts.

9) VIEW NOTICES AND COMMUNICATIONS FROM SIAOA

SIAOA posts official notices, circulars, and announcements on MyGate:

Step 1: Open MyGate > Tap "Notice Board" or "Community" section.
Step 2: Browse recent notices. Categories include:
  - General Announcements
  - Maintenance Schedules (water tank cleaning, power shutdown, fumigation)
  - Meeting Notices (AGM, EGM, Board Meetings)
  - Rule Changes or Reminders
  - Event Announcements
Step 3: Tap on a notice to read the full details.

You will also receive push notifications for important notices. Keep MyGate notifications enabled to stay informed.

For archived notices or if you cannot find a specific communication, contact the SIAOA admin office at admin@siaoa.co.in.

11) MYGATE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS FOR SOBHA INDRAPRASTHA

Q: I am not receiving MyGate notifications. What should I do?
A: Check that notifications are enabled for the MyGate app in your phone settings (Settings > Apps > MyGate > Notifications > Enable). Also ensure you are logged into the correct account linked to your Sobha Indraprastha flat.

Q: My domestic help's QR code is not working at the gate.
A: The QR code may have expired or the schedule may have changed. Go to MyGate > My Daily Help > select the staff member > regenerate the code or update the schedule. If the issue persists, contact SIAOA security.

Q: I sold/rented my flat. How do I transfer MyGate access?
A: The outgoing owner/tenant should deactivate their MyGate account for the flat. The new owner/tenant should register on MyGate and request the SIAOA admin to link their account to the flat. Contact admin@siaoa.co.in for the transfer process.

Q: Can I approve a visitor's entry from outside India?
A: Yes. MyGate works over the internet. As long as you have the app installed and are logged in, you can approve or deny entry requests from anywhere in the world.

Q: How do I add a second family member to MyGate for my flat?
A: Go to MyGate > Settings > "My Flat" or "Family Members" > Tap "Add Member" > Enter their phone number. They will receive an invitation to join your flat on MyGate.

Q: The security guard is not using MyGate for my visitors.
A: Report this to SIAOA management at bom@siaoa.co.in. All visitor entries at Sobha Indraprastha are required to go through MyGate as per SIAOA security policy.

Q: Can I see a history of all visitors who came to my flat?
A: Yes. Go to MyGate > "Activity" or "Visitor Log" to see a timestamped history of all approved and denied entry requests for your flat.

Q: How do I update my phone number on MyGate?
A: Go to MyGate > Settings > Profile > Update Phone Number. You will need to verify the new number via OTP. If you face issues, contact MyGate support from within the app or ask SIAOA admin for help.
